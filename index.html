Order from the factory, or buy off the lot?

What's the real difference?

Lot: you take a unit that's in stock or already in transit, and you can drive it home this week or within a couple of weeks. You're limited to the colors and options the dealer picked, which can include dealer add-ons.

Factory order: you pick the trim, color and options, and the dealer places an allocation request. You wait for it to be built and shipped. Timing depends on the brand and the dealer's allocation, so get a written estimate. You only pay for what you chose.

Which is better for each of these three?
  • 2026 Passport: buy off the lot. Honda has already moved to 2027 pricing, so a new order would almost certainly be a 2027 at 2027 prices. The 2026s on the lot are the ones to negotiate on. Scott Honda has RTL, Towing, TrailSport Blackout and Elite units on hand.
  • 2027 Telluride: lot is fine, but check the build date. About 35 units are in stock locally. Because TSP+ only covers builds after June 2026, a fresh factory order guarantees a qualifying build. On the lot, check the door-jamb sticker on each unit. Order only if you want a specific color/trim combination that isn't in stock.
  • 2026 4Runner: consider ordering or reserving an allocation. In-stock units tend to be higher trims or carry dealer packages (like Del Toyota's $2,375 DPP). The cheapest SR5 is still in transit (10/23) and two more are in build (Dec). Ordering or reserving a pipeline unit is how you avoid add-ons. The IIHS rear-seat flag applies either way.
Does ordering get a better price?

Not automatically. Ask the dealer to put the agreed price in writing before you leave a deposit: MSRP or discount, doc fee, and an explicit "no add-ons" line. Rebates and APR specials are tied to the delivery date, not the order date. Current offers expire 11/2/2026, so an order that arrives later may get different incentives.

Deposit: what should I ask?
  • Is it fully refundable, and is that in writing?
  • Is the price locked if MSRP rises before delivery?
  • Can I refuse the car if it arrives with add-ons I didn't agree to?
  • For Telluride: will the build date be after June 2026? (Any new order will be.)
Lot checklist before signing
  • Read the Monroney (window sticker) and compare it to the published MSRP. Ask about any gap (e.g., Passport RTL listed $100 under, TrailSport Blackout $355 over).
  • Check the build date on the door jamb (essential for Telluride).
  • Run the VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov.
  • Bring the infant seat and do a rear-facing fit test, including front-passenger legroom with the seat installed.
  • Get an out-the-door quote by email: price + $490 doc + 6% PA tax + title/tags, with no add-ons.
